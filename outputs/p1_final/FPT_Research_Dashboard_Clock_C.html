Valuation assumptions and source register

risk free anchor. Analyst VND long-duration benchmark, 4.50%. Not a verified 23 Nov close. HNX 9 Nov trading statistics show ten-year domestic trades spanning 3.38%-4.5032%; this dispersion is not a par curve. Sensitivity covers the valuation consequence.

total equity premium. Analyst total VND equity premium of 8%, including local equity/country risk. Do not add a separate country premium to this input. No empirical beta/ERP estimate is claimed.

beta. Judgemental beta 1.0, not a regression. WACC case adjustments are explicit risk stresses.

target debt weight. Exogenous long-run financing mix, not a market-cap weight derived from an unverified quote. This avoids price/WACC circularity.

cost of debt. Analyst marginal nominal VND debt cost 5.5%, compared with forecast financing costs. Not an issuer commitment or observed new loan.

tax. 20% normalised terminal and interest-tax-shield assumption. Explicit forecast retains case tax rates and incentive uncertainty.

shares. 1,885,727,648 issued shares from 25 Sep 2026 issuer notice already visually verified in repository source register. Add a separate 1% unissued dilution reserve. No additional announced issue is asserted. Future bonus shares are neutral and not separately forecast.

associate pe. Analyst capitalisation multiple on 2027 FPT-attributable associate earnings, not a peer median or quoted FTEL multiple. This captures reinvested earnings as well as distributions. Do not add associate book value or future dividends again.

nci. Forecast NCI book equity times 1.0-2.0 proxy because subsidiary market values are unavailable. This is a model risk. Earnings-share allocation would understate the substantial opening NCI capital.

timing. Forward value at projected 31 Dec 2026 balance sheet. Q4 cash movements are in that balance sheet, so exclude Q4 FCFF. Annual FCFF uses mid-year timing 0.5-4.5. Gordon terminal stream also uses mid-year timing with terminal discount at 4.5, not 5.0.

terminal. 2032 NOPAT uses 2031 EBIT, case g and normalised 20% tax. Reinvestment = NOPAT*g/ROIC, including operating cash needs. No extra CIP/PPE asset addition. Immediate stable-state transition is an explicit simplification.

cash. Subtract required operating cash from projected year-end cash. Deduct subsequent annual increases in the reserve from FCFF. A below-floor balance remains a negative equity adjustment; no financing plug.

price. No verified same-cutoff closing quote. Upside and market-implied reverse DCF remain unavailable. Hypothetical price probes are labelled as such. No comparison to the old Clock B price.
