# FPT Clock B Investment Committee Memo

**Valuation date:** 9 October 2026  
**Market price:** VND57,900 per share  
**Blended fair value:** VND94,498 per share  
**Recommendation:** BUY  
**Risk:** HIGH

## Decision

Open or retain a measured position only if the mandate accepts high cash-conversion risk. The 63.2% valuation gap is large, but the next position increase should require evidence that the H1 working-capital outflow reverses by FY2026.

## Why the stock is potentially mispriced

The current price is approximately 10.9x trailing earnings and 38.7% below its level one year earlier, while 8M2026 revenue and profit before tax grew 13.5% and 17.5%. H1 new signed revenue increased 32.3%, Japan revenue grew 24.4%, and AI and data-analytics revenue grew 54%. The market appears to be pricing a sharper structural slowdown than the disclosed demand indicators currently show.

## What changed from Clock A

Clock A valued the fully consolidated group at 31 October 2025. From 1 January 2026, FPT Telecom moved to the equity method. Clock B therefore does not compare the new group revenue and FCFF mechanically with the old series.

The new model:

- values the continuing consolidated operations with an FCFF DCF;
- capitalises 2027E associate earnings at 14.0x;
- uses 1,885.7 million post-bonus shares;
- raises Base WACC to 10.9% from 10.2%;
- reduces the starting EBIT margin to 14.8%;
- increases near-term working-capital investment;
- uses a 2027E P/E cross-check rather than the stale 2025 peer snapshot.

The Clock A and Clock B per-share values should not be read as a clean target-price bridge because the accounting perimeter, market date and share count changed simultaneously.

## Base-case underwriting

Revenue grows from VND53.1 trillion in 2026E to VND89.5 trillion in 2031E. EBIT margin recovers from 14.8% to 16.3%, while FCFF rises from VND4.9 trillion to VND11.1 trillion. The model assumes that capex intensity declines gradually and working-capital investment normalises after 2026.

The SOTP produces VND97,511 per share. A 15.0x multiple on 2027E EPS produces VND89,978. The blended value is VND94,498.

## Variant view versus the market

The investment case does not require a return to Clock A's 20x comparable multiple. The Base P/E cross-check uses 15x, while the observed trailing market multiple is 10.9x. The variant view is that double-digit revenue growth, margin recovery and associate earnings deserve a mid-teens multiple once the new reporting basis is understood.

## Primary risk

H1 operating cash flow was negative VND1.15 trillion despite VND5.05 trillion of parent net profit. The FY2025 forecast review also showed that capex and working capital caused most of the FCFF miss. If the H1 cash outflow does not reverse, the Base case overstates distributable cash and the apparent valuation gap narrows materially.

## Position and monitoring rules

- Start below full target size because the live monitor is amber and cash conversion is red.
- Add only after FY2026 operating cash flow confirms a substantial second-half reversal.
- Hold the Base case while revenue growth remains at least 13%, EBIT margin remains at least 14.7% and new signed revenue grows at least 20%.
- Re-underwrite if revenue growth falls below 10%, EBIT margin falls below 14%, or capex exceeds 6.5% of revenue.
- Reduce or exit if two or more live indicators are red or if the refreshed blended value loses its required margin of safety.

## Evidence required next

1. FY2026 operating cash flow and the receivables, payables and deferred-expense bridge.
2. FY2026 capex split between maintenance and growth projects.
3. Revenue and profit contribution from FPT Telecom under the equity method.
4. Conversion of H1 signed revenue into recognised Global IT revenue.
5. Margin progression in Japan, domestic IT and AI-related work.
