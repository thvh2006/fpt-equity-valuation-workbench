# Clock B Valuation Note

## Purpose

Clock B is the current research view at 9 October 2026. It is a new underwriting cycle rather than a revision of Clock A. Clock A remains frozen at 31 October 2025 for out-of-sample evaluation.

## Accounting-basis change

From 1 January 2026, FPT Telecom moved from full consolidation to the equity method following a change in governance rights. FPT now records its share of FPT Telecom profit in the associate line. Parent net profit and EPS were not affected by the presentation change, but consolidated revenue, EBIT, cash flow, assets, debt and non-controlling interests are no longer directly comparable with FY2025.

Clock B therefore uses a sum-of-parts structure:

1. value the continuing consolidated operating business with an FCFF DCF;
2. capitalise the separately forecast share of associate and joint-venture profit;
3. add cash and short-term financial investments;
4. subtract reported debt and non-controlling interests;
5. divide by the post-bonus share count.

The associate book value is shown for reference but is not added to the bridge because associate earnings are already capitalised. This prevents double counting.

## Evidence cutoff

- Market price: VND57,900 per share at 9 October 2026.
- Shares outstanding: 1,885.7 million after the September 2026 bonus issue.
- Latest reviewed financial statements: six months ended 30 June 2026.
- Latest operating update: eight months ended 31 August 2026.
- Vietnam 10-year government bond yield: approximately 4.58% at the valuation date.

## Forecast design

The 2026 revenue anchor annualises the official 8M2026 revenue of VND35.4 trillion. The 2026 EBIT margin is anchored to H1 EBIT before financial items, calculated as gross profit less selling and administration expenses.

The Base case assumes:

- revenue growth moderates from 13.5% in 2026 to 9.0% in 2031;
- EBIT margin recovers from 14.8% to 16.3%;
- capex falls from 5.2% to 4.5% of revenue;
- working-capital investment normalises from 15% of incremental revenue in 2026 to 5% thereafter;
- share of associate profit grows from 10% in 2027 to 6% in 2031.

The Downside and Upside cases use the same operating build and one authoritative case selector. The selector changes revenue growth, margins, reinvestment, associate-profit growth and valuation inputs while source actuals remain unchanged.

## Cost of capital

The Base WACC is approximately 10.9%. It uses a 4.58% risk-free rate, an 8.35% equity risk premium, a 0.90 levered beta, a 4.25% pre-tax cost of debt and market-value capital weights. The terminal growth rate is 4.0% in the Base case.

Explicit FCFF covers 2027-2031 and uses a mid-year discount convention. The terminal value is measured at 31 December 2031 and discounted for 5.22 years from the valuation date.

## Base-case result

- Operating enterprise value: VND130.7 trillion.
- Capitalised associate earnings: VND43.8 trillion.
- SOTP value: VND97,511 per share.
- 2027E P/E cross-check: VND89,978 per share.
- Blended fair value: VND94,498 per share.
- Upside to market price: 63.2%.
- Mechanical recommendation: BUY.
- Risk label: HIGH.

The blended value gives 60% weight to the SOTP DCF and 40% to a 2027E P/E cross-check. The recommendation is mechanical and should not be read without the risk monitor.

## Main live risk

H1 operating cash flow was negative VND1.15 trillion while parent net profit was VND5.05 trillion. This is the only red quantitative monitor indicator. It could be seasonal, but the FY2025 backtest also identified capex and working-capital use as the main FCFF forecast errors. The model therefore treats cash conversion as the principal evidence required to confirm the thesis.

## Decision rules

- Keep the Base case while revenue growth, margin and signed revenue remain at or above their green thresholds.
- Re-underwrite working capital and capex after the FY2026 cash-flow statement.
- Reduce the valuation if cash conversion remains below 30%, if revenue growth falls below 10%, or if EBIT margin falls below 14%.
- Preserve Clock A and Clock B as separate artifacts; never backfill Clock A with later evidence.

## Limitations

- The 2026 full-year forecast annualises eight-month revenue and six-month margin evidence; seasonality may differ.
- The associate earnings line includes associates and joint ventures beyond FPT Telecom.
- Current peer-company estimates were not licensed for redistribution. The P/E cross-check uses an explicit analyst multiple rather than a refreshed proprietary peer table.
- The H1 cash-flow result is not annualised because working-capital timing can reverse in the second half.
